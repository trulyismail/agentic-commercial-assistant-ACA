ACA vs. a RAG chatbot — answering questions isn't running the workflow

A "custom RAG chatbot over your documents" is the most common thing an AI agency ships, because it is genuinely the easiest thing to build well. It is also a different product from ACA, and the difference is the whole workflow around the single Q&A step.

Generic RAG chatbotACA
Classifies an inbound leadNoYes, with a confidence threshold that escalates low-confidence cases to a human
Retrieves from a knowledge baseYes — its whole jobYes — one worker among several, hybrid dense+sparse
Enriches with company researchNoYes, cached
Drafts a full commercial proposalNo — answers a question, does not compose a documentYes
Self-critiques its own draftNoYes, capped at one rewrite
Escalates an unanswered question to a human, and stages the web answer for reviewNo — usually just says it doesn't knowYes — the veille worker + FAQ staging
Writes to a CRMNo, typicallyYes, after human approval
Needs a paid vector databaseOftenNo — pgvector optional, an in-memory fallback otherwise
