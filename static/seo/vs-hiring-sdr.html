ACA vs. hiring an SDR — a tool that prepares, not one that decides

The realistic alternative to ACA is usually not another piece of software — it is hiring a junior SDR to triage the inbox. That is a legitimate option, and this page states plainly where ACA is not a substitute for a person, not just where it is cheaper.
