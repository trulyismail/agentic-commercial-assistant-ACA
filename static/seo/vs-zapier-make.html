ACA vs. Zapier / Make — the orchestrator isn't the judgement

"Can't I just build this in Zapier?" is a fair question, and the honest answer is: you can wire the plumbing in Zapier, but Zapier will not do the thinking. A Zapier automation is a fixed sequence of steps triggered by an event; it has no judgement about whether a lead is worth a fast reply, no memory of what was said to that sender before, and no way to say "I don't actually know the answer to this question" instead of inventing one.

Zapier / MakeACA
Reads and classifies the emailNo — a keyword filter at bestYes — a language model, with a confidence score
Researches the sender's companyNo, unless you build and pay for a separate stepBuilt in, cached, free-tier
Answers from your own knowledge baseNoYes — hybrid dense + sparse retrieval over your FAQ
Drafts a personalised replyNoYes, with a self-critique pass before a human sees it
Knows when it doesn't knowN/AYes — flags the gap instead of guessing
Stops for human approval before actingOnly if you build that step yourselfArchitectural — cannot be skipped
